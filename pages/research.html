GPCR structural biology

We combine cryo-electron microscopy, molecular pharmacology, computational modeling, and molecular dynamics to resolve how ligands reshape receptor conformations and control downstream signaling.

  • Ligand recognition and receptor activation
  • Allosteric regulation and signaling bias
  • GPCR–transducer complex assembly
Background, strategy & findings +

Scientific question How does ligand recognition reorganize conserved GPCR microswitches to produce a defined transducer-coupling state?

Research strategy Cryo-EM structure determination is integrated with mutagenesis, cellular pharmacology, molecular modeling, and molecular dynamics simulation.

Key findings We map ligand-specific contacts, activation-associated helix movements, and determinants of GPCR–transducer assembly.

DREADD tool development

We use structure-guided receptor engineering to develop DREADDs with precise ligand selectivity, signaling profiles, and cellular control—building better tools for dissecting neural circuits and disease biology.

  • Structure-guided receptor design
  • Selective designer ligand discovery
  • Neural-circuit and cell-type control
Background, strategy & findings +

Scientific question Can receptor activation and ligand selectivity be rationally reprogrammed without compromising signaling control?

Research strategy Structural analysis, receptor engineering, ligand screening, and functional assays are combined in iterative design cycles.

Key findings Structure–function relationships guide receptor variants and selective chemogenetic ligands with defined signaling profiles.

Neurological drug development

We translate GPCR mechanisms into therapeutic hypotheses, integrating virtual screening, pharmacological profiling, and functional assays to discover and optimize compounds for neurological disorders.

  • Structure-based virtual screening
  • Cellular pharmacology and selectivity profiling
  • Lead discovery for neurological disease
Background, strategy & findings +

Scientific question Which receptor conformations and binding-site features can be translated into selective therapeutic hypotheses?

Research strategy Structure-based virtual screening is connected to cellular pharmacology, selectivity profiling, and mechanism-focused validation.

Key findings Integrated screening prioritizes tractable ligands and reveals testable structure–activity relationships for neurological targets.
