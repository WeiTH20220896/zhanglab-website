Mechanistic and therapeutic insights into class A GPCR dimers in neuropsychiatric disorders

Zhaojing Wang, Linlin Wang & Shicheng Zhang

npj Drug Discovery 3, Article 17 (2026)

This review integrates evidence for class A GPCR dimerization with structural mechanisms, signaling pharmacology, and strategies for therapeutic targeting in neuropsychiatric disorders.

First author

Zhaojing Wang is a PhD student who joined Zhang Lab in the 2024 cohort. Her research focuses on GPCR oligomerization and molecular pharmacological mechanisms.
