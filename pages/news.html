Congratulations to Liu Rongxi and Ming Lang

Zhang Lab celebrates Liu Rongxi and Ming Lang on completing the undergraduate stage of their six-year program.

Liu Rongxi explored computation-guided chemogenetic tool development, while Ming Lang studied the analysis and development of chemogenetic tools. This graduation marks an important milestone—not a farewell. Both will continue their training and research with Zhang Lab.

May this milestone renew your curiosity and confidence as we continue exploring the questions ahead—together.

With pride and warm wishes from everyone at Zhang Lab.

June 3, 2026Publication

New review in npj Drug Discovery

2024-cohort PhD student Zhaojing Wang is first author of our newly published review, “Mechanistic and therapeutic insights into class A GPCR dimers in neuropsychiatric disorders.”

The article synthesizes experimental evidence for class A GPCR dimerization, explains how dimer assembly reshapes receptor signaling, and discusses emerging strategies for dimer-informed therapeutics in neuropsychiatric disorders.

Congratulations to Zhaojing and all authors on this important milestone.
