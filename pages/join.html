Join Us

Work across GPCR structural biology, DREADD tool development, and neurological drug discovery in a collaborative research environment.

Zhang Lab is continuously recruiting talented individuals passionate about GPCR neuropharmacology

Our core directions are GPCR structural biology, DREADD chemogenetic tool development, and neurological drug discovery. We value careful experiments, open discussion, and research that connects mechanism with therapeutic possibility.

Open Positions

Postdoctoral Fellow

Requirements
  • Hold or be about to receive a PhD in pharmacology, biochemistry, chemical biology, computational biology, or a related field; generally under 35 years of age and in good health.
  • Have published high-level research papers as first author.
  • Applicants for structural biology positions should have substantial experience in structure determination and analysis.
  • Able to independently design research projects, conduct experiments, perform data analysis, and write manuscripts.
  • Assist with other research-related tasks assigned by the supervising PI.
Compensation & Benefits
  • Salary: "Boxin" postdoctoral fellows receive approximately CNY 410,000/year pre-tax (including housing fund); "Boya" postdoctoral fellows approximately CNY 300,000/year; National Category B postdoctoral fellows approximately CNY 280,000/year; standard full-time postdoctoral fellows approximately CNY 210,000/year. Outstanding fellows may receive year-end performance bonuses after one year. Base salary increases by CNY 20,000/year in the third year. PIs may provide additional project compensation for outstanding fellows.
  • Housing: Postdoctoral fellows may apply for on-campus housing based on their start date, with rent at market rates.
  • Funding: Fellows are encouraged to apply for various talent programs and research grants, including the National Postdoctoral Program, Hong Kong Scholars Program, Macao Young Scholars Program, NSFC, Beijing NSF, and China Postdoctoral Science Foundation.
  • Household registration: Non-Beijing postdoctoral fellows may transfer their household registration to Peking University Health Science Center. Spouses and children may follow upon completion of the postdoctoral term if residency conditions are met.
  • Children's education: Eligible postdoctoral fellows may enroll their children in local kindergartens, primary schools, and middle schools with an official introduction letter.
