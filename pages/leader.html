Biography

Dr. Zhang Shicheng has long been engaged in GPCR neuropharmacology research. By combining molecular pharmacology approaches with cryo-electron microscopy (Cryo-EM) structural determination, his lab systematically dissects the molecular pharmacological mechanisms underlying complex GPCR signaling networks. The lab also develops novel GPCR chemogenetic tools, biosensors, and drug screening platforms, providing mechanistic insights and drug candidates for both basic GPCR research and disease treatment.
